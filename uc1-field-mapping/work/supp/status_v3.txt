===== 5. What Actually Happened
WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED - THE DELAY EXPLAINED | 
SHORT VERSION | 
Position | Phase 1a was due in Q3 2026 and has not been delivered. Bronze ingestion for Op1 and FreeWheel landed on time in July. What has not landed is a finalised MVP definition, the pipeline Bronze tables, and the forecast feed. We have been defining requirements and building transforms at the same time. In Jira, UC1 sits at ~3% complete (CARL-1364) and its Silver / Gold epics are at 0 story points complete.
WHY IT SLIPPED | 
1. Requirements not finalised | The request is 101 metrics and attributes, many not needed for high-level Executive / PSky reporting; 63 are flagged for v1. There was no finalised MVP or complete documentation from the business. CARL (Arlene Rose, Mike Hodge) meets Jason Chalmers and Adam Christensen multiple times a week to document requirements, formulas and business logic field by field. So far only iDeal fields are confirmed; Op1 / WRR and Salesforce fields are not.
2. Pipeline Bronze not available | Pipeline reaches Bronze via Oracle, not directly from Salesforce - a design choice. The Salesforce / Metropolis epic (CARL-2778) is 73% complete, but pacing-domain ingestion is still held, so the pipeline Bronze tables are not yet available to the Silver team. Mike Hodge is building the pipeline query directly in Oracle in the meantime.
3. No forecast feed | Finance forecast is one of the three primary converged metrics. The template is agreed with the business; the feed has not been provided. There is no Jira ticket tracking it.
WHAT HAS ALREADY BEEN DONE TO SAVE TIME | 
Design shortcuts | Skip the Cube semantic layer for MVP. Pull Linear revenue from MicroStrategy Bronze instead of iDeal direct (which waits for Venus). Pull Digital revenue from WRR Bronze (CARL-597, done) instead of Op1 direct.
Scope reduction | Arlene proposed cutting the MVP to only the fields needed for Executive / PSky reporting and Finance Pacing, adding drillable attributes sprint by sprint. Jason agreed and is narrowing the list. This is the key input we are waiting on.
WHY IT DID NOT REACH THE SPONSOR | 
Workarounds absorbed the impact | Jason stayed engaged with CARL and, in parallel, built temporary fixes for Allie. Those kept Sales functioning, so the slip never surfaced as visible pain at working level and was not escalated.
Sponsor absorbed the cost | Allie paused report modernisation over the summer expecting CARL, then went into a PSKY / Paramount meeting without it, and is heading into acquisition activity in the same position.
What we own | We relied on the working level to surface a slip that the working level had absorbed. The Q3 position should have gone to the sponsor directly. Several critical items - forecast feed, MVP field list, allocation - were never tracked in Jira, which made the gap harder to see.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT | 
1 | Lock the reduced MVP field list with Jason.
2 | Get dates for pipeline Bronze (Debo, CARL-2778) and the forecast feed (business).
3 | Publish a delivery date only once 1 and 2 are known - a date we hold, not an aggressive one.
4 | Treat Bobby V (Option C) as a caveated interim view only; it uses iDeal / Op1 directly rather than the agreed MicroStrategy / WRR sources.
5 | Create Jira tickets for every untracked dependency so status is visible on the board.
6 | Fortnightly sponsor-level status so the next slip reaches Allie from us.

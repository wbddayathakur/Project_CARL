BUSINESS OBJECTIVE | 
Goal | Single trusted executive view of Booked Revenue + Pipeline + Forecast aligned to Finance methodology, replacing the weekly Excel deliverable.
Problem | Sales and Finance run parallel reporting. No drill-down, no on-demand slicing, weekly-only cadence, recurring 'whose number is right?' reconciliation.
Primary Users | Executive Ad Sales Leadership (Allie Carr, Jenn Blue+); Sales VPs; Ad Sales Finance; Pricing & Planning
READINESS ASSESSMENT (AS OF TODAY) | 
Phase 1A - Booked + Pipeline | ~65-70% ready. Design and requirements complete; blocked on iDeal mapping, BU definition, allocation automation, grain/SCD decisions.
Phase 1B - Forecast + Sports | ~20-25% ready. Requirements complete; blocked on Finance forecast feed (schema/cadence/access) and Sports methodology decision.
Phase 2 - Expanded Access | Not started. AE-level RLS, multi-version forecasting, embedded Teams/SharePoint views, rate-card analysis.
KEY MESSAGE FOR LEADERSHIP | 
Bottom Line | Remaining risk is concentrated in business-rule, ownership and Finance dependencies - not in CARL platform capability. The converged model and foundational ingestion exist; execution is gated by a defined set of decisions.
Top 3 Asks | 1) Unblock iDeal field mapping  2) Secure a stable Finance forecast feed commitment  3) Lock BU definition and North Star grain/SCD decisions

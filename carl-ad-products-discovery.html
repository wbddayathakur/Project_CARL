The report's headline metric is wrong for two compounding reasons

139 formulas across the four Product Info sheets filter Data!$B:$B for "Infornt" — a misspelling of "Infront" that never appears in the data. Those sheets feed the yield adjustment term on the quarter sheets, so the error lands directly in Yield $ and Yield %.

The same formulas also filter Data!$G:$G = "Sports". That column holds free-text sponsorship package names such as "2026 March Madness | Custom" and can never equal "Sports". Correcting the spelling alone still returns $0. Both need an SME decision on the intended value before any reconciliation tolerance is meaningful.

Ad Innovation is a reclassification, not incremental revenue

The bucket identity on the quarter sheets is Q = G + (E − I) + (M − (G − I)), which reduces algebraically to E + M. Ad Innovation revenue is already contained within DAT and Traditional Digital. Summing the three headline buckets naively overcounts by exactly the Ad Innovation figure — $3.22M on Entertainment 1Q alone. This is PRD risk #6, and it is why the dedup identity must be an asserted test rather than a convention.

Yield is the CPM premium of Ad Innovation over that Traditional baseline:

Yield $ = ((AdInnoRev − ProductInfoAdj) − ((AdInnoImps / 1000 − ImpsAdj) × TradCPM)) / 1e6

The ProductInfoAdj term is the one corrupted by DQ-1.

4Data quality findings

Twelve findings from the extraction. Five are critical: they change reported numbers today, or they will fail an Iceberg load. All line references are to the Week 35 workbook.

Defect register
IDSeverityFindingEvidenceImpact
DQ-1Critical Misspelled criteria literal "Infornt" in 139 formulas All four Product Info sheets, e.g. 1Q Product Info!E20 and !CW20, filter Data!$B:$B for "Infornt". Data!B only ever contains "Infront". Counts: 36 / 35 / 34 / 34 by quarter. Every "Max Sports" line returns $0. Product Info feeds the yield adjustment on the quarter sheets, so the error propagates into Yield $ and Yield %.
DQ-2Critical Predicate G="Sports" can never match its column Data!G holds free-text sponsorship package names — 49 blanks plus 7 values like "2026 March Madness | Custom", "2026-27 NFL Regular Season | Hard Knocks - In-Season". It never equals "Sports". Correcting DQ-1 alone still returns $0. The Sports carve-out is structurally dead. "Total x Sports" identifies sports by whether a package name is blank, so sports classification has no data model behind it.
DQ-3Critical Unmapped dimension value silently resolves to 0 Data row 34 — Virtual Product Placement / Other-Intl / Uncategorized Revenue / Production Fee. "Production Fee" is absent from the buy-type map, so XLOOKUP returns 0 and the mapping key becomes "Virtual Product Placement-Entertainment-0". $108,507 is flagged Include but is dropped by every SUMIFS that filters buy type. It is in the total and absent from every breakdown.
DQ-4Critical Net revenue and gross revenue are summed together ALL AD INNO REVENUE!C8 = SUMIFS(Data!$BF…) + SUMIFS('DISPLAY DATA'!$U…). Data!H6 is "Net Revenue" (Operative); DISPLAY DATA!E7 is "Total Gross Revenue (Adjusted)" (GAM). The two are added in one expression. Every product line that has both Operative and GAM revenue mixes revenue bases. Overstates the combined figure by the gross-to-net difference on the GAM portion.
DQ-5Critical Text sentinel "$0" in a numeric measure column DISPLAY DATA!AJ8 = IFS(AG8="ADIN", E8+G8, AG8="DAT", E8, AG8="TRAD", "$0") — the TRAD branch returns the string "$0", not the number 0. Confirmed present in AS8 and BB8 as well. Works by accident in Excel, which coerces text to 0 inside SUM. A typed decimal column in Iceberg will reject the row or null it.
DQ-6Material Case and spelling variants across criteria literals "HBO MAX" in 5 casings; "Streaming" in 3 including "STraming"; also ENT/Ent, INCLUDE/Include, TRUE/True, NEWS/News, Sports/SPORTS, Rotational/rotational, Sponsorship/sponsorship, plus a stray "ENTS". Harmless in Excel, which matches text case-insensitively. In Spark or Trino the comparison is case-sensitive, so each variant becomes a silent zero. The single largest lift-and-shift risk.
DQ-7Material 4,472 IFERROR wrappers mask errors instead of raising them 21.4% of all formulas. Concentrated in the Product Info sheets (659–681 each) and quarter sheets (113–132 each). A broken reference renders as 0 or a dash, indistinguishable from a genuine zero. No way to tell a real $0 from a failed lookup.
DQ-8Material Hard-coded 1.9× impression multiplier in 560 formulas Data columns AI, AL, AO, AR, AU, AX, BA, BD each carry =IF($B7="Infront",J7*1.9,J7). Raw In-front impressions 757,821,180 become 1,439,860,243. 682,039,063 impressions injected by a constant with no effective date, owner or rationale in the data. Keyed on the same text literal that is misspelled in DQ-1.
DQ-9Material Stale entries in the product → format map from past renames Data!CW:CX still carries Homepage Discovery, Max Brand Suitability and Shop With HBO Max, none of which exist in the data. The data instead has HBO Max Brand Suitability and Shop HBO Max, appended as new rows. Live evidence of PRD risk #3. Products were renamed and the map was appended to rather than updated, so it now holds both old and new names with no effective dating.
DQ-10Material Inconsistent and unbounded aggregation ranges Bounds 7..100, 7..83, 7..115 and 8..161/165/168/180/184/187 coexist against 56 actual data rows. A further 6,379 SUMIFS use whole-column references such as Data!CK:CK. No current loss — the block is small enough. Latent silent truncation the moment data outgrows the tightest bound, and whole-column refs will absorb header and total rows.
DQ-11Housekeeping Live #VALUE! errors in the GAM site map DISPLAY DATA!AF resolves via XLOOKUP($C8,BL:BL,BM:BM,#N/A). Rows 75–77 return #VALUE! for ad units Ad Exchange Display, INTL_CNNI_FW_MMP, INTL_CNNi_TEST; row 73 returns 0. Those rows drop out of every vertical filter. Dollar exposure is trivial today — $109.90 — but the mechanism is unguarded.
DQ-12Housekeeping Untracked manual override recorded only in a cell comment Comment on AJ9 across two sheets, author Jamie Jackette: "Deleted 2M per Bridget". A $2M adjustment exists only as prose. Invisible to any consumer and unreproducible.
One inconsistency to confirm rather than assert

In DISPLAY DATA!AJ8, the ADIN branch returns E8+G8 — the non-audience and audience revenue columns — while the DAT branch returns E8 alone, which is the non-audience column. Given that DAT is audience-targeted inventory, the DAT branch appears to reference the wrong side of the pair. This needs Jamie to confirm intent before it is either ported or fixed.

The structural blocker: both existing reports are crosstabs, not extracts

In Data, quarter and audience flag are encoded in header rows 3 and 5, not as columns — the same three measures repeat 8 times across columns H through AE. DISPLAY DATA does the same in rows 5 and 6. 82% of GAM revenue cannot be attributed to an ad product

Of $80.38M GAM display revenue, $65.74M across 60 rows sits under the product value "Other" and is bucketed as Traditional by default via IF(B="Other","Exclude","Include"). Only 13 rows carry a real product: RE/FORM $14.14M, News Brand Suitability $0.35M, Native $0.16M.

This is the structural reason the PRD defers GAM to P2. If a premium format runs in GAM inventory there is currently no field that identifies it, so it is silently counted as traditional display. L-2 must add a product or placement classification at the line-item level, otherwise AP-3.3 (yield by ad product) cannot be completed for display.

One reordering to propose

PRD v0.2 carries reconciliation and data quality only as acceptance criteria on other stories. Given that five defects in the current model change reported numbers or would fail a typed load, the reconciliation baseline itself is unsound. Data quality should be promoted to its own P0 story so that "reconciles to the Excel model" does not become a requirement to faithfully reproduce known errors.
